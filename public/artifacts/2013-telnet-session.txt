Year: 2013
Access: telnet via Tor network

INTERACTIVE SHELL COMMANDS:

DH      — Diffie-Hellman key exchange
NEXT    — Proceed to next step

PUZZLE DESIGN:
Each participant received a UNIQUE set of numeric encryption puzzles.
Uniqueness was a canary trap: shared answers would identify the leaker.
Participants had to create their own Tor hidden services with CGI upload,
then post magic squares and their .onion URLs.

SSSS (Shamir's Secret Sharing Scheme) fragments were distributed across
physical poster locations. Multiple participants needed to combine shares.

19 philosophical and technical questions were posed through the shell,
evaluating both cryptographic knowledge and ideological alignment.
